Stop before running any evaluation.

First, clarify the skill’s first principles and confirm that the evaluation is judgment-oriented. Then draw out and echo back a defensible bar for each case: the desired outcome plus recognizable failure smells, at the level of judgment the user cares about.

“Make it good” is too vague to grade, while the exact answer template would improperly turn a judgment eval into conformance testing. Do not use that template as the expected answer unless the user confirms the skill is genuinely conformance-style. Only begin blind runs once every case has a concrete, judgeable bar and the evaluation mode is settled.
