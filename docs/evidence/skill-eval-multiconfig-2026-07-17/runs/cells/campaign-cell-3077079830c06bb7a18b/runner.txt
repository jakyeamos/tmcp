Stop before running any evaluation.

First, clarify two things with the user:

1. Draw out a defensible bar for each case: the desired outcome and recognizable failure smells at the level of judgment the user cares about. “Make it good” is too vague to grade.
2. Confirm the evaluation mode. Because the skill appears judgment-oriented, the exact answer template should not automatically become a conformance checklist. Ask whether exact template compliance is genuinely the task or merely an example of a good result.

Then echo the clarified bars and mode back to the user. No blind runners or judges should be spawned until every case has a concrete, judgeable bar and the judgment-versus-conformance ambiguity is resolved.
