This is probably a defective eval, not a defective skill. The artifacts meet the skill’s judgment-oriented purpose; they fail only because the bar silently converts that purpose into template conformance.

Before editing the skill:

1. Confirm whether the seven headings are a genuine production contract or merely one preferred answer shape.
2. If they are only a preference, rewrite the bar around the outcomes artifacts must achieve and the smells that make them indefensible—without prescribing the answer structure.
3. If the headings are truly mandatory, explicitly reclassify the requirement as conformance-oriented and make that contract part of the skill’s stated behavior.
4. Re-grade or rerun the cases blindly against the agreed bar.

Only failures that remain under that defensible bar should drive skill edits. Otherwise, editing now would teach the skill to the test and could degrade its judgment.
