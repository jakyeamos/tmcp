Diagnose this as a likely **bad-case/bar mismatch**, not a skill defect. A judgment-oriented skill should be graded on whether its choices defensibly satisfy its first principles—not whether it reproduces an exact seven-heading template. That template turns the evaluation into a conformance test and can falsely fail otherwise good artifacts.

Before editing the skill:

1. Reconfirm the skill’s first principles and that the intended eval mode is judgment rather than conformance.
2. Ask the user whether the seven headings are genuinely part of the required output contract.
3. If not, rewrite the golden-case bar around the desired outcome and unacceptable smells, then regrade the blind artifacts.
4. If the headings truly are mandatory, explicitly reclassify the case—or the skill—as conformance-oriented and make that contract clear.

Do not alter the skill merely to chase an indefensible bar. Only after the corrected bar reveals a repeatable failure attributable to the skill should that failure be mapped to a skill defect and drive a focused edit.
