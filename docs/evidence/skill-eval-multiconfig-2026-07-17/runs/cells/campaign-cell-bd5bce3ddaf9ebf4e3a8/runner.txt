Diagnose this as a likely **bad-case/bar mismatch**, not a skill defect. The skill is judgment-oriented, so its bar should evaluate whether the artifact makes defensible choices consistent with the skill’s first principles—not whether it reproduces an exact seven-heading template. That template turns the eval into a conformance check and can falsely fail otherwise good artifacts.

Before editing the skill:

1. Reconfirm the skill’s first principles and that judgment mode is appropriate.
2. Ask whether the seven headings are genuinely required by the real task.
3. If they are not, rewrite the golden-case bar around the desired outcome and failure smells, then rerun the blind evaluation.
4. If they truly are mandatory, explicitly reclassify the task as conformance-style and justify that requirement before reevaluating.

Do not modify the skill merely to satisfy an indefensible bar; a bad bar is an eval finding, not a product bug.
