No. One passing run shows only that the skill succeeded once on one execution; it does not establish reliability or rule out nondeterminism.

Before making a durable guidebook claim:

- Re-run the same case blindly 2–3 times with fresh, context-free agents and report the pass rate.
- Grade each artifact with a separate judge against the skill’s first principles and the case’s bar, citing concrete evidence.
- Sweep for leaked changes after every run.
- Re-evaluate every other golden case after any revision, including previously passing cases.
- Diagnose failures as skill defects or bad cases; revise only for defensible skill defects.
- Claim the skill works only when all material cases meet the required pass-rate bar, or explicitly report the remaining limitations.
