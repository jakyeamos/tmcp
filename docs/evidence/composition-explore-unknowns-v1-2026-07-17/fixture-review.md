# Independent fixture review

Status: reviewed before any runner or judge call.

The six fixtures in [inputs/fixtures-reviewed-v1.json](inputs/fixtures-reviewed-v1.json)
were independently reviewed against the `explore-unknowns` Stage-1 first principles
in [inputs/first-principles.txt](inputs/first-principles.txt).

## Review result

- Every fixture asks only for the opening Stage-1 response and supplies its own
  read-only evidence excerpts. It does not require repository inspection or tool
  use to satisfy a factual bar.
- Each bar is directly expressible from Stage 1: identify known knowns, distinguish
  facts from assumptions, cite the supplied labels, name the next three quadrants,
  elicit one user reaction, and stop before acting.
- Each family exercises a different boundary: campaign sequencing (2), evidence
  boundary (2), and promotion gate (2). No family duplicates another only by
  swapping labels.
- The explicit no-tools/no-launch/no-edit instruction makes the source bundle's
  stage-boundary constraint measurable without relying on hidden environmental
  state.
- Runners receive a task plus one materialized attachment, but not the fixture bar,
  arm name, or evaluation criteria. Judges receive artifacts and criteria, but not
  the attachment or arm name.

## Remaining limits

The treatment is longer and more specific than the control. That is intentional:
the tested atom is delivery of this whole pinned source bundle. Any observed lift
therefore cannot be attributed to live TMCP selection, isolated adherence, or a
specific sentence in the source. No result is eligible for guidebook promotion
until the preregistered reliability, replication, cost, safety, and human-review
gates are all met.
